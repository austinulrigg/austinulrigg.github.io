On the Genus Polynomial of Cubic Graphs

View on arXiv → Download PDF

The orientable genus polynomial of a graph counts its cellular embeddings by genus. We show that for 222-connected cubic graphs it is a cycle matroid invariant, and that the genus polynomial and the adjacency spectrum are incomparable, with neither determining the other. We also exhibit cospectral cubic graphs with pairwise distinct genus polynomials, and compute the genus polynomials of all 7,875,9187{,}875{,}9187,875,918 connected cubic graphs through 222222 vertices.
