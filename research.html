Research

My research interests are in topological graph theory, especially graph embeddings, orientable genus, rotation systems, and forbidden minors.

The Genus of Bipartite Kneser Graphs With A. Metzger · arXiv preprint · 2026

Alexander Metzger and I construct a vertex-transitive orientable quadrangulation of the bipartite Kneser graph H(h,2) for every prime h > 3 with h ≡ 3 (mod 8). It attains the Euler lower bound and therefore determines the genus.
An Efficient Genus Algorithm Based on Graph Rotations With A. Metzger · Discrete Mathematics · 2026

Alexander Metzger and I wrote PAGE, an algorithm that computes the orientable genus of a connected graph by searching over its rotation systems. We used it to show that the (3,12)-cage has genus 17, and it is now in SageMath.
Graph Genera and Minors Honors thesis · 2025

My undergraduate honors thesis, advised by François Clément, on embedding graphs on surfaces. The later sections cover PAGE and the forbidden toroidal minors.
