Rotation Systems

A rotation system picks a cyclic order of the edges at each vertex, and this determines an embedding of the graph on an orientable surface. Here you can change the rotations of K3,3K_{3,3}K3,3​, K5K_5K5​, K5∖eK_5 \setminus eK5​∖e, or the octahedral graph and see what faces and surface you get.

Each face is cut open into a polygon. Sides with the same letter are the two darts of one edge.

4Building the surface

Glue two faces along an edge they share, u→v to v→u. When one polygon is left, its sides are identified in pairs. This is a fundamental polygon of the surface.

The same embedding in 3D. The faces are traced, cut open, and placed on the surface. The surface comes from Alexander Metzger's live solver (genus.fly.dev, used with permission).
