Toroidal Obstructions with No K5 Minor

View on arXiv → Download PDF Supplemental data and code →

Finding the complete set of forbidden minors for the torus remains an open problem. We determine the complete list of toroidal minor obstructions with no K5K_5K5​ minor and prove that there are exactly 747474, each with between 101010 and 141414 vertices. Combined with the known classification of toroidal obstructions with no K3,3K_{3,3}K3,3​ minor, this determines every toroidal obstruction that excludes one of the forbidden minors for planarity. The proof uses three-vertex cuts to show that, apart from K3,7K_{3,7}K3,7​, every three-connected obstruction in this class has two distinct independent triples, each the common neighborhood of at least two degree-three vertices. We consider the three possible sizes of the intersection of the two triples. In each case, planarity tests reduce the proof to a finite enumeration. From the 747474 minor obstructions we also obtain a complete list of 381381381 topological obstructions for the torus with no K5K_5K5​ minor.
