Toroidal Obstructions with No K5 Minor

View on arXiv → Download PDF Supplemental data and code →

Finding the complete set of forbidden minors for the torus remains an open problem. We determine the complete list of toroidal minor obstructions with no $K_5$ minor and prove that there are exactly $74$, each with between $10$ and $14$ vertices. Combined with the known classification of toroidal obstructions with no $K_{3,3}$ minor, this determines every toroidal obstruction that excludes one of the forbidden minors for planarity. The proof uses three-vertex cuts to show that, apart from $K_{3,7}$, every three-connected obstruction in this class has two distinct independent triples, each the common neighborhood of at least two degree-three vertices. We consider the three possible sizes of the intersection of the two triples. In each case, planarity tests reduce the proof to a finite enumeration. From the $74$ minor obstructions we also obtain a complete list of $381$ topological obstructions for the torus with no $K_5$ minor.
