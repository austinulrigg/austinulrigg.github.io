Resolving Pisanski’s Conjecture

View on arXiv (link pending) Download PDF

We prove Pisanski's conjecture on quadrilateral embeddings of regular bipartite graphs. For integers m≥d≥4m\geq d\geq4m≥d≥4 with 4∣md4\mid md4∣md, we construct both a connected simple ddd-regular bipartite graph GGG with mmm vertices in each bipartition class and an orientable quadrangulation. This graph's genus is γ(G)=1+m(d−4)4. \gamma(G)=1+\frac{m(d-4)}4. γ(G)=1+4m(d−4)​. These quadrangulations attain the lower bound on the genus from Euler's formula. We give dihedral constructions for even mmm and a cyclic construction for every m≥dm\geq dm≥d with 4∣d4\mid d4∣d. In each case, two cyclic permutations of a connection set of size ddd determine all of the local rotations.
